# Data and shared split

Previously published workbooks are preserved in source/phishing.xlsx and source/phishing_split.xlsx. Split: train 7,738 (4,310 legitimate, 3,428 phishing); validation 1,658 (923 legitimate, 735 phishing); test 1,659 (924 legitimate, 735 phishing). Result=1 means legitimate and Result=-1 phishing. Features are 30 categorical codes; zero is not missingness. The split has duplicate vectors and conflicting labels as discussed in the report.

Additional local CSV copies and feature-schema files have not been published by this directory-only change.
